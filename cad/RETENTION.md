# CAD retention — finished models only

Owner decision (2026-11-05): this repository keeps finished models and
the specific hardware parameters needed to reproduce them. Iteration
ladders, fit-test prints, rejected revisions and measurement-process
trees are not retained; the fitting work is done and its results are
the parameters in the finished models.

Kept: acrylic node concept (current physical direction), conductor
terminal-fresh-v6 and sensor-deck-v7 (latest of their lines), speaker
border-v3, panel hole measurements (hardware dimensions), grid-fit
layout models and their build scripts.
Removed in this change: conductor v2-v5 iterations and concept files,
speaker enclosed-v2/carriers-v1/box-reference, temporary panel joints
(rejected), print-validation test kit, panel-box prototype, speaker
fit/track studies, and the presentation concept renders. Removed trees
remain in this repository's history and in the private archive.
