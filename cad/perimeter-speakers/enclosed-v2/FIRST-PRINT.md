# First single-speaker fit test — enclosed v2

Print one copy of each of the four STL files in `parts/`. They are already oriented for printing. Use 100% scale, 50% gyroid infill and 5 wall loops in your Bambu printer profile. The T rear cover requires supports; inspect its underside and nut pockets in the slicer. No file here is pre-sliced G-code.

Prepare four M3 × 40 screws, four M3 nuts and small washers; check the real washer/thread stack. Add thin compliant foam pads at rigid front/rear casing contact points. There is room for a loose 80 × 26 × 5 mm fibre pad behind the speaker. Do not obstruct moving parts or any existing speaker vents.

Read README.md for assembly sequence. Fit the actual speaker and cable before tightening; test grille clearance, retention and whiteboard clearance. CAD fit does not establish hardware fit. Print the twin only after this single passes.
